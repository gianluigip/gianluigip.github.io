Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

home-lab rclone is a personal backup tool used only by its owner, Gianluigi Pierini, to copy data from his own home server to his own Google Drive account.

What it accesses

It requests only the drive.file scope, which limits it to files it creates itself. It cannot see any other file in the Google Drive account.

What it collects

Nothing. It has no users other than its owner, collects no personal data, uses no analytics or cookies, and shares no information with anyone. Everything it uploads is encrypted on the owner's server before it leaves, with a key that only the owner holds.
